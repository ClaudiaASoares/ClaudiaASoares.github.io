I love to be thrown into the webs and flows of trying to solve real-world problems with human-centered machine learning. It's amazing how these truly human problems poke us to develop new algorithms and theories that are both mathematically sound and practically useful.

I develop methods for trustworthy machine learning, focusing on optimisation and decision-making under constraints and uncertainty. My work spans distributed learning and geometric models, with applications in space operations, clinical decision support and facial animation.
