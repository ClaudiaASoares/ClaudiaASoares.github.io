I develop estimation and optimisation methods for space operations, with machine learning used for orbit prediction and information support. Conjunction analysis addresses uncertainty in the positions of orbiting objects. Satellite-network research studies topology decisions over time.

This topic also includes language models and retrieval-augmented generation for operational documents, and OrbitZoo as a benchmark for reinforcement learning in orbital systems.

The papers below connect these methods with data quality, atmospheric density forecasting and collision-risk prediction. Work on orbital dynamics and conjunction data also examines how physical and statistical models can inform the use of machine learning.
