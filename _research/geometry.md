My work in geometric learning uses geometric priors to construct models for 3D data. SCENE-Net studies interpretable 3D segmentation through Group Equivariant Non-Expansive Operators (GENEOs), vision-language fusion, and probabilistic shape modelling from incomplete observations.

In facial animation, I study optimisation methods for inverse rigging and the estimation of blendshape controls. This connects the geometry of a facial model with the computation needed to produce animation.

This work is applied on medical image-text retrieval, and infrastructure inspection using point clouds of electrical transmission systems.

## Facial-motion generation

STEmo is a novel dataset for emotion transition learning in the space of face blendshape representations. The dataset records facial motion during transitions between emotion categories, with generation baselines used to study how these transitions can be reproduced.

This line of work also examines how facial-motion generation and preview can be integrated into Unreal Engine workflows for MetaHuman characters.
