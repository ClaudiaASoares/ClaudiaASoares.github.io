My work in clinical decision support studies how information from patient records and clinical guidelines can inform a clinician's decision process. The HyCARE research direction combines clinical information extraction with guideline-based decision support, with uncertainty in model outputs examined through evaluation.

I also develop models for healthcare management, including referral network analysis and emergency-department demand prediction. This work uses network models and time-series forecasting to study how patients move through services and how demand changes over time. 

Causal explanations for graph neural networks provide methods for examining model predictions. Work on medical vision-language representations evaluates image-text retrieval.
